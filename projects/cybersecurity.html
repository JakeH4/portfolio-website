Cybersecurity Pen Testing Forensics

A collection of cybersecurity projects completed as part of CSCI 3403 at the University of Colorado Boulder. These projects provided hands-on experience with real-world security techniques and tools.

Work included penetration testing engagements, cross-site scripting (XSS) exploitation and defense strategies, and forensic analysis of compromised systems. Each project emphasized both offensive and defensive security perspectives.
