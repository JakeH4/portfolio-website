Slim Stow Rooftop Cargo Carrier

Product Design Market Research

Designed and developed a rooftop cargo carrier product with a focus on market differentiation. This project explored the intersection of product design and business strategy.

The Slim Stow concept addresses the need for a low-profile, aerodynamic rooftop cargo solution that minimizes drag while maximizing storage capacity. Research involved competitive analysis, user interviews, and iterative prototyping.
